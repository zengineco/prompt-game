Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 June 2026 · PROMPT (Reverse Mode), a Zengine™ game

This Policy explains what data PROMPT (Reverse Mode) ("the Game"), operated by Zengine™, collects and how we use it. We collect as little as possible and we do not sell your data.

We do not collect your email, password, payment details, IP address (Discord proxies requests), real name, or location. We request only the Discord identify scope.

2. How We Use It

Solely to operate the Game: place you in a session, display your name and submissions to other players in that session, tally votes, and keep score. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA/UK, we process your data on the basis of your consent (you choose to launch and authorize the Game) and our legitimate interest in operating the gameplay you requested. You may withdraw consent by leaving the Game and requesting deletion (section 7).

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Game data (players, submissions, votes, scores) is stored in Supabase, our hosting and database provider, which acts as our data processor. Supabase servers are located in the United States. If you are outside the US, your data is transferred to and processed in the US. Access is restricted by row-level security; only the Game's server logic can write data.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Discord — provides authentication and the platform the Game runs on. See the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — database and hosting (data processor). See Supabase Privacy.
  • Google AdSense — our public web pages (such as this one) include Google AdSense, which may set cookies to serve and measure ads. The Game itself, inside Discord, does not display ads. See Google's ad policies. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings.

6. Data Retention

Game data is tied to a game session and kept only as long as needed to run and review play. Session data may be periodically cleared. You can request deletion of your data at any time (section 7).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (GDPR for the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA for California, and similar laws elsewhere), you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the data we hold about you, and to object to processing. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. To exercise any right, contact us at admin@f-keys.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The minimum age to use the Game is 13, consistent with Discord's requirements. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use row-level security on all database tables, restrict writes to server-side logic, and transmit data over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be shown by an updated effective date here.
